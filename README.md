# 🌹 IT Workplace Language Guide

> **Understand what IT people say — and what they actually mean.**

🌐 **[Live Website](https://it-workplace-language-guide.netlify.app/)**

The **IT Workplace Language Guide** is a practical reference for people entering or working in IT environments.

In IT workplaces, people don't always communicate using direct technical language. A simple sentence like:

> **"What's on your plate?"**

doesn't mean someone is asking about food. 😄

It usually means:

> **"What tasks are you currently working on?"**

This guide helps bridge that gap between **everyday workplace language** and **actual IT meaning**.

---

## 🎯 What This Guide Covers

The guide is organized into **30 sections**, with **10 entries per section**.

### 🗣️ Workplace Communication

Learn common phrases used by managers, developers, designers, QA engineers, project teams, and other IT professionals.

Examples:

- What's on your plate?
- Can you take this up?
- Keep me posted.
- Loop me in.
- Any blockers?
- What's the ETA?
- Who owns this?
- Let's circle back.
- Do you have bandwidth?
- Does that make sense?

Each phrase explains the difference between its **literal meaning** and its **actual workplace meaning**.
